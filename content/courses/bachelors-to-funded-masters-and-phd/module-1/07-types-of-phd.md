# What Are the Different Types of PhD and Doctoral Degrees?

There are several doctoral pathways. Their names, structures and admission requirements differ across countries and universities.

## 1.7.1 Traditional Research PhD

This is the most common form of PhD. Students undertake an independent research project under the guidance of one or more academic supervisors. They produce a thesis that demonstrates original research and makes a contribution to existing knowledge. The programme may include research training, seminars and occasional coursework, but the main requirement is the completion of the research thesis.

## 1.7.2 Structured or Coursework PhD

Some universities offer structured doctoral programmes. These combine formal coursework, research methods training, seminars and independent research. Students complete the required courses before or alongside their main research project. This format is common in countries such as the United States and in some universities in Europe and Asia.

## 1.7.3 Professional Doctorates

Professional doctorates are designed for experienced professionals who want to conduct advanced research within their professions or industries. Examples include the Doctor of Education (EdD), Doctor of Business Administration (DBA), Doctor of Public Health (DrPH), Doctor of Nursing Practice (DNP), Doctor of Engineering (EngD) and Doctor of Pharmacy (PharmD).

Professional doctorates often combine coursework with applied research. They differ from a traditional PhD in their professional focus, although both involve advanced doctoral-level study.

## 1.7.4 Practice-Based or Creative PhD

This pathway is common in creative and artistic disciplines. Candidates conduct research through creative practice, such as visual arts, music, design, film, theatre or writing. The final submission may combine a creative work or practical project with a written thesis explaining the research, methods and contribution to knowledge.

## 1.7.5 Integrated Master's and PhD

Some universities offer programmes that combine Master's-level research training with doctoral study. Students complete an initial period of advanced coursework or research training before progressing to the PhD stage. This pathway is sometimes called an integrated PhD or an MRes/PhD route. Progression to the doctoral stage may depend on satisfactory academic performance and research assessment.

## 1.7.6 Industrial or Collaborative PhD

An industrial PhD involves research conducted in collaboration with a company, government agency or other external organisation. The research addresses a problem relevant to the partner organisation while meeting the university's academic requirements. Candidates often receive funding or employment support through the partnership.

> **Important:** These pathways are not available at every university. Applicants should check the specific doctoral qualifications offered by their chosen institutions.
