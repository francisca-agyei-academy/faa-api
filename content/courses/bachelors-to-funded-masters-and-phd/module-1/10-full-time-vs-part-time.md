# What Is the Difference Between Full-time and Part-time PhD Study?

A full-time PhD requires a greater level of commitment to research. Candidates usually dedicate most of their working time to their doctoral studies. A part-time PhD allows candidates to combine research with employment, professional responsibilities or other commitments. It generally takes longer to complete.

| Full-time PhD | Part-time PhD |
| --- | --- |
| Greater weekly research commitment | Reduced weekly research commitment |
| Usually shorter completion period | Usually longer completion period |
| Often suitable for scholarship-funded students | Often suitable for working professionals |
| May involve residency or campus attendance requirements | May offer greater scheduling flexibility |

> International student visa conditions may restrict part-time study. International applicants should check visa rules before choosing a study mode.
