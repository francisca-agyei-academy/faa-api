# The CAREERS Framework: Choosing a Master's Programme for Professional Career Development

If your goal is to enter the professional world, advance your career or develop expertise in a particular industry, choosing the right Master's programme requires careful consideration.

The **CAREERS Framework** provides seven assessment areas that prospective students should consider when selecting a Master's programme with professional career ambitions. The framework examines career relevance, applied skills development, real-world experience, employer connections, employment prospects, recognition and career mobility, and scholarships and financial considerations. Your programme selection should reflect your career ambitions, the skills employers value, and the professional opportunities available after graduation.

The framework helps prospective students answer seven fundamental questions:

## a) Career relevance

- Does the programme provide knowledge and qualifications relevant to your intended profession?
- Is the programme recognised and valued within your intended industry?
- Does the programme provide access to recognised professional qualifications or certifications?

## b) Applied skills development

- Does the programme equip you with the practical and professional skills required in the workplace?
- Does the curriculum incorporate practical training, professional projects or industry-based assignments?

## c) Real-world experience

- Does the university provide opportunities to gain practical workplace experience during your studies?
- Does the university assist students in accessing internships and industry placements?

## d) Employer connections

- Does the university provide career services and support for your transition into the professional world?
- Are students exposed to professional networks and potential employers?
- Does the programme have partnerships with relevant industries and employers?

## e) Employment prospects

- What employment opportunities are available to graduates of the programme?
- Do graduates secure employment in their intended professional fields?
- What career advancement opportunities does the qualification provide?

## f) Recognition and career mobility

- Does the programme provide access to recognised professional qualifications or certifications?
- Is the qualification recognised in other countries?
- Does the programme develop internationally transferable skills?
- Are there opportunities for international internships or professional placements?
- What work visa and post-study employment pathways exist in your intended study destination?

## g) Scholarships and financial considerations

- Are university scholarships available?
- Are there government-funded study opportunities?
- Does the programme offer tuition discounts or financial assistance?
- What are the living expenses in the study destination?
- Are there restrictions on working while studying?
