# The RARAF Framework: Choosing the Right Master's Programme If You Intend to Pursue a PhD

If you are yet to complete your Master's degree, your programme selection deserves careful consideration. Your decision should reflect both your immediate academic interests and your longer-term research ambitions. Choosing a Master's programme requires more than considering the university's reputation, programme ranking, or tuition fees. It is important to assess whether the programme provides the academic, research, professional, and financial support needed to achieve your long-term goals.

The **RARAF Framework** provides six key considerations to guide this decision: Research opportunities, Academic supervision, Research methodology training, Research environment, Academic progression and Funding opportunities. These considerations help prospective students evaluate how well a programme aligns with their research interests, academic ambitions and financial circumstances before making an application decision.

The framework helps prospective students answer six fundamental questions:

## i) Research opportunities

Does the programme offer a dissertation, independent research project, or opportunities to participate in research activities?

## ii) Academic supervision

Are there academics whose research interests align with your intended area of specialisation?

## iii) Research methodology training

Does the programme provide opportunities to develop qualitative, quantitative or mixed-method research skills?

## iv) Academic and research environment

Does the university provide access to research centres, laboratories, academic seminars, conferences and research networks?

## v) Funding opportunities

Do the university and programme provide clear pathways towards doctoral study? Are scholarships, tuition support, research assistantships or other funding arrangements available?

> **NB:** Applicants should examine whether the programme requires an independent thesis, a minor research project or a substantial dissertation. This distinction matters when applying for doctoral opportunities.

Selecting the right Master's programme is essential for preparing for future doctoral studies. Prioritise programmes that offer substantial research opportunities, relevant academic supervision, research methodology training and a supportive research environment. A programme with a strong research component will help you develop the knowledge, skills and experience needed to strengthen your future PhD applications.
